Notary prices can range from a small state-regulated fee for a basic in-person notarization to a higher total when travel, multiple signatures, witnesses, or an online service are involved. The most useful way to compare costs is to look at the total price for your exact document—not just the advertised notary fee.

How much does a notary cost in 2026?

There is no single nationwide price for every notarial act. Traditional notary fees are often limited by state law and may be charged per signature, oath, or notarial act. A mobile notary may also charge a separate travel or convenience fee when allowed and properly disclosed.

What can make a notarization cost more?

  • Multiple signatures or seals: Some documents require more than one notarized signature.
  • Witnesses: A document may require one or more witnesses in addition to the notary.
  • Travel: A mobile notary may charge a separately agreed travel fee.
  • Urgent or after-hours appointment: Some providers charge for special scheduling.
  • Document-specific requirements: Real estate, estate planning, or international documents may involve extra steps.

How to compare online and in-person notary costs

Start by confirming whether the document needs a notary, how many signatures must be notarized, and whether witnesses must be present. Then compare the complete cost of a local notary appointment with the online platform's final fee. Include travel time, transportation, appointment availability, and any required printing or mailing.

Ways to avoid unexpected notary fees

  1. Ask the receiving organization exactly what type of notarization it accepts.
  2. Count notarized signatures and required witnesses before booking.
  3. Ask a mobile notary for a written total, including travel.
  4. Check the online provider's current pricing page before uploading sensitive documents.
  5. Do not pay for notarization until you understand whether the document also needs witnesses, signatures, or legal review.

Does notarization include legal document preparation?

Usually, no. A notary generally verifies identity, administers an oath when required, and completes the notarial certificate. Notarization does not automatically draft a will, power of attorney, travel consent letter, or other legal document, and it is not a substitute for legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an online notary cost?

Proof / Notarize lists $25 for the first notary seal, with possible additional fees for extra seals, witnesses, or signers. Check current checkout pricing.

Is a mobile notary more expensive?

A mobile notary may charge a state-limited notarial fee plus a separately disclosed travel or convenience fee, depending on applicable rules.

Is the notary fee charged per document?

It depends on state rules and the notary's fee structure. Ask whether the price is per signature, notarial act, certificate, or document.

Does notarization include legal advice?
